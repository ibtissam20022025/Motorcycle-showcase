About MOTO X

MOTO X began as three friends who raced on weekends and rebuilt engines on weeknights.

Every motorcycle they bought felt like a compromise. It was either fast and brutal, or comfortable and dull. So they stopped buying and started building, one frame at a time, in a single rented garage.

Today the atelier is a team of engineers, painters, leather cutters and test riders. The garage is bigger. The rule is the same: nothing leaves the floor until someone on the team has ridden it hard and come back grinning.

What we stand for

  • Every gram justified. If a part doesn't make the bike faster, safer or more yours, it doesn't go on.
  • Ridden, not just tested. Sensors tell us numbers. Riders tell us the truth. We listen to both.
  • Built to be kept. Full parts traceability and lifetime support, so your machine outlives the trend.

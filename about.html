002 / The Origin

A Garage,
An Obsession

The first Nocturne prototype, 2014

MOTO X began as three friends who raced on weekends and rebuilt engines on weeknights.

Every machine they bought felt like a compromise. It was either fast and brutal, or comfortable and dull. So they stopped buying and started building, one frame at a time, until other riders started asking for one of their own.

Today the atelier is a team of engineers, painters, leather cutters and test riders. The garage is bigger. The rule is the same: nothing leaves the floor until someone on the team has ridden it hard and come back grinning.

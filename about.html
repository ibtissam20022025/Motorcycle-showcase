MOTO X Performance

Built by riders

MOTO X began as three friends who raced on weekends and rebuilt engines on weeknights. Every motorcycle they bought felt like a compromise, so they started building their own in a single rented garage. Today the atelier is a team of engineers, painters, leather cutters and test riders, and the rule is still the same: nothing leaves the floor until someone has ridden it hard and come back grinning.
